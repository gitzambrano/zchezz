# v5 NNUE research ledger — updated 2026-09-18

This is the canonical short ledger for the current v5 NNUE investigation. Treat `v5.06` as the only valid v5 baseline until a candidate passes a large direct gate. Never promote from a short screen alone.

## Baselines

- v5 family baseline: **v5.06**, NNU4 HalfKP-4Bucket `2560 -> 48/perspective -> 20 -> 1`, weight SHA256 `5e6cca42f11823505edefb3ee068e0e9bb3a8576f8fb5b0b7a688736ed377150`.
- primary 3.x family on `main`: **v3.30**.
- Cross-family comparisons are diagnostic only. Any v5 successor must beat v5.06 directly.

## Root-probe teaching: valid mechanism, old objective rejected

The root-probe teacher enumerates legal children, ranks them cheaply, then searches selected children independently. It is operational and produces dense child-ordering labels.

The original clean v5.06 root-probe `b0` screen looked very strong at 64 games: **30W/19D/15L = +82.97 Elo**. A proper independent gate invalidated it:

- 400 games / 200 paired openings, disjoint opening offsets.
- `b0` vs v5.06: **112W/159D/129L**, 47.875%, **-14.77 Elo**.
- paired-bootstrap CI95: **[-40.13, +10.43] Elo**.

Layer-scope ablations and adaptive/deeper root-probe also selected **epoch 0** under the old policy/ranking/trust objective. Conclusion: root-probe itself remains useful, but the old distillation objective is not a robust strength direction.

## NNU5 / larger-network diagnosis

The frozen v5.09 NNU5 was rematerialized on v5.06 search as diagnostic v5.21, isolating the network from later search changes.

Architecture: HalfKP-8Bucket, `5120 -> 192/perspective -> 32 -> 1`, ~1.98 MB weights. Original training used 2.4M phase-balanced positions and converged offline.

Fixed-node H2H removes throughput as the main explanation for its weakness:

- v5.21 vs v5.06 at 200k nodes/move: **15W/2D/23L = -70.44 Elo**.
- v5.21 vs v3.28 at 200k nodes/move: **12W/1D/27L = -136.97 Elo**.

Status: **capacity scaling rejected in the present training formulation**. More parameters/data and slightly better teacher move agreement did not translate into alpha-beta strength.

## Static-evaluation calibration diagnostic (v5.22)

On 480 independent positions against pinned Stockfish 19 at 16,384 nodes:

- v5.06: MAE **162.12 cp**, RMSE **273.51**, Pearson **0.8246**, near-even MAE **87.23 cp**.
- v3.28: MAE **141.21 cp**, RMSE **249.05**, Pearson **0.8779**, near-even MAE **63.03 cp**.

A single affine rescale does not close the gap. v5.06 phase slopes also vary strongly by material. Conclusion: the v5.06 weakness is not merely one output-scale constant; useful evaluation structure/ranking is missing or poorly learned.

## Historical QAT/runtime mismatch (v5.23)

Historical NNU4/NNU5 QAT did not reproduce the C runtime's discrete `>>8` activation equations. Direct measurement on real v5.06 weights over 3,000 positions found:

- L1 code mismatch: **32.45%**.
- L2 code mismatch: **34.54%**.
- score MAE training-forward vs runtime: **18.97 cp**.
- RMSE **23.36 cp**, p95 **44.45 cp**, max **67.34 cp**.
- **66.5%** of positions differed by more than 10 cp.

This mismatch exists in v5.06 too, so it does not by itself explain the NNU5 regression. It nevertheless means historical training optimized a materially different evaluator from the one used by search.

## Runtime-exact QAT causal test (v5.24) — closed

Branch: `exp/v524-exact-qat-rootprobe`, run `35052864357`.

`ExactRuntimeNNUE` reproduced the C evaluator with **0 mismatches in 256 positions** before training and **0/128** after export. The runtime-exact implementation is therefore validated infrastructure.

Repeating the old b0 root-probe objective with exact QAT still selected **best_epoch = 0**. No trained epoch beat the original v5.06 weights under that objective. The exported checkpoint is effectively v5.06.

Its 96-game screen was **29W/43D/24L = +18.11 Elo**, CI95 **[-32.67, +69.68]**, but because best_epoch=0 this is explicitly a noise/control result, not learned strength.

Conclusion: **keep runtime-exact QAT, reject the old b0 objective**.

## Global-to-L2 side channel (GLB2) — closed/rejected

Branch: `exp/v507-global-l2`, run `35046251975`.

Design: freeze v5.06 NNU4 and add a 31->20 global projection into L2 (~620 int8 weights). Zero-init evaluator equivalence passed.

All three feature sets selected **best_epoch = 0**, so no learned projection survived validation. The additional runtime path nevertheless reduced bench throughput from about **2.28M NPS to 1.71-1.72M NPS** (~25% penalty).

96-game screens, therefore essentially v5.06 plus overhead:

- all-global: **22W/37D/37L**, **-54.74 Elo**, CI95 [-108.33, -3.62].
- aggregate/material: **30W/32D/34L**, **-14.48 Elo**, CI95 [-69.68, +39.99].
- relational: **19W/39D/38L**, **-69.68 Elo**, CI95 [-124.50, -18.11].

Status: **rejected**. Do not gate or promote any GLB2 variant.

## Centered search-delta microcorrection (v5.25) — closed/control

Branch: `exp/v525-centered-delta`, run **`35138319132`**.

The target discarded absolute teacher calibration and tried to learn only the per-parent centered root-probe residual. Three variants shared the same 5,000-position corpus:

- `d25`, L2+L3: **best_epoch=0**, rejected without H2H.
- `d50`, L2+L3: **best_epoch=0**, rejected without H2H.
- `d25l3`, L3 only: selected epoch 1 by an objective improvement of only ~3e-9 and produced a 96-game screen of **34W/40D/22L = +43.66 Elo**, paired CI95 **[+3.62,+84.89]**.

The positive `d25l3` screen is **not learned strength**. Exact post-hoc inspection proved the exported evaluator is behaviorally identical to v5.06:

- all 20 deployed L3 int8 weights are identical to v5.06;
- only 2 bytes differ in the 248,020-byte NNU4 file: the ignored epoch metadata byte and one byte of the float L3 bias;
- the effective L3-bias change is only about **-0.0004 cp**;
- exhaustive evaluation of every possible integer L3 sum in the physical range (**325,121 sums**) found **0 integer-score differences** after runtime scale, clamp and rounding.

The v5.06 and `d25l3` bench also visited exactly the same 3,940,069 nodes at depth 12. Therefore the +43.66 Elo screen is an empirical identical-engine control showing how noisy short movetime H2H can be. **No 400-game gate was launched.**

Status: **closed**. `best_epoch > 0` is not sufficient; runtime-effective difference must be proven before H2H.

## Direct discrete L3 CP fit (v5.26) — closed/rejected

Branch: `exp/v526-direct-l3-fit`, run **`35139498244`**.

This removed SGD/STE entirely. L1/L2 were frozen, exact runtime L2 codes were extracted, and the 20 deployed L3 int8 weights were solved directly by ridge regression against centered root-probe CP residuals.

The method did produce real nonzero integer weight changes and improved held-out CP RMSE, but teacher top-1 agreement became worse and Elo did not improve:

- `g25r01`: held-out RMSE **+1.414% better**, top-1 **45.96% vs 47.81% baseline**; 96-game H2H **28W/36D/32L = -14.48 Elo**, CI95 **[-69.68,+39.99]**.
- `g25r10`: held-out RMSE **+1.323% better**, top-1 **46.13% vs 47.81%**; H2H **25W/43D/28L = -10.86 Elo**, CI95 **[-51.03,+29.02]**.
- `g50r01`: held-out RMSE **+1.382% better**, top-1 **43.43% vs 47.81%**; H2H **29W/38D/29L = 0.00 Elo**, CI95 **[-43.66,+43.66]**.
- `g25r0`: unregularized normal equations were singular; diagnostic only.

All three learned ridge candidates passed export, perft, UCI and 128-position runtime-exact parity. No architecture overhead was added.

Conclusion: **centered CP RMSE is not a useful optimization proxy here**. The direct discrete machinery is valuable infrastructure, but the CP-regression objective is rejected. None of the v5.26 candidates deserves a large gate.

## Discrete ranking repair (v5.27) — closed/rejected

Branch was `exp/v527-discrete-ranking-repair`, workflow run **`35168730151`**.

This kept L1/L2/L3 bias frozen and searched the 20 deployed L3 int8 weights directly against exact runtime ranking/search-utility. Three settings (tight, balanced, aggressive) all converged to the baseline vector:

- **0/20 changed weights** in every setting.
- held-out top-1 gain: **0.0000**.
- no H2H was run.

Conclusion: **v5.06 is a one-step discrete local minimum in L3 for this ranking objective**.

## Multi-coordinate L3 beam escape (v5.28) — closed/rejected

Branch was `exp/v528-l3-beam-escape`, workflow run **`35169010064`**.

A deterministic beam search tested coordinated L3 perturbations beyond the one-step local minimum. It found real deployable alternatives (for example, budget-4 changed 3/20 weights and budget-8 changed 5/20), but none improved held-out top-1:

- baseline held-out top-1: **50.84%**.
- budget-2: **50.84%**.
- budget-4: **50.84%**.
- budget-8: **50.84%**.
- held-out top-1 gain in all cases: **0.0000**.

No candidate reached H2H. Conclusion: **the 20-weight L3 projection is representationally exhausted for small search-informed corrections**.

## Sparse runtime-exact L2 ranking (v5.30) — closed/rejected

Branch: `exp/v530-sparse-l2-ranking`, workflow run **`35405131966`**.

This moved the same exact search-utility objective one layer earlier without changing architecture or NPS: L1, L2 bias and all of L3 were frozen; only existing L2 int8 weights could change. A teacher-best-vs-baseline-best influence scan found **1,482 active L2 coordinates**. Three train-only coordinate pools were then searched exactly:

- tight: top 64 coordinates, budget 2, max coordinate delta 1;
- balanced: top 128, budget 4, max delta 2;
- wide: top 192, budget 8, max delta 3.

Despite many coordinates having positive local influence proxies, **every exact ranking search converged at step 0**. No single +/-1 L2 weight perturbation in any pool improved the full training objective:

- selected step: **0** for all three variants;
- changed deployed L2 weights: **0/1920**;
- held-out top-1 gain: **0.0000**;
- no export/H2H was run.

Conclusion: **v5.06 is also a strict one-step discrete local minimum in L2 for the current ranking/search-utility objective**. This does not yet prove that coordinated sparse L2 changes cannot help.

## Paired sparse-L2 escape (v5.31) — closed/rejected

Branch: `exp/v531-paired-l2-escape`, workflow run **`35405318089`**.

This tested whether the one-step L2 local minimum from v5.30 could be escaped by coordinated exact integer changes while keeping L1, L2 bias and all of L3 frozen. Candidates had total L1 distance two from v5.06:

- +/-2 on one selected L2 weight; or
- +/-1 on two distinct selected L2 weights.

All selection before the final shortlist used only the train parent split. Three coordinate pools were tested:

- tight: 24 coordinates, **1,152** exact pair candidates;
- balanced: 32 coordinates, **2,048** candidates;
- wide: 40 coordinates, **3,200** candidates.

No configuration found a pair that improved the full training objective. In fact, the best train candidate in every search was already worse than v5.06:

- baseline train objective: **0.414137**, top-1 **49.39%**;
- balanced best train candidate: **0.420705**, top-1 **49.02%**;
- wide best train candidate: **0.418982**, top-1 **49.02%**;
- tight best train candidate: **0.422647**, top-1 **49.02%**.

Held-out selection also did not rescue the candidates. The selected balanced pair changed 2/1920 L2 weights but worsened held-out top-1 from **51.18% to 50.84%** (gain **-0.34 pp**). The selected wide pair reached **51.01%** (gain **-0.17 pp**) and also worsened the combined objective. All variants had `learned=false`; no export/H2H was run.

Conclusion: **small local post-training edits are exhausted under this root-probe ranking objective**. v5.27-v5.31 jointly reject one-step L3, coordinated L3 beam, one-step L2, and total-distance-two L2 corrections. This does not prove that arbitrary/global L2 retraining cannot help; it shows that local integer micro-surgery does not expose a generalizing direction.

## Established experimental rules

1. 48-96 game H2H is triage only; it can fluctuate by many tens of Elo, even for behaviorally identical evaluators.
2. Epoch 0 must always be an eligible checkpoint. `best_epoch > 0` is also insufficient: the exported runtime evaluator must be proven behaviorally non-equivalent to baseline.
3. Verify deployed integer weights / binary payload before spending H2H budget; metadata-only or sub-rounding changes are controls, not candidates.
4. Use paired openings and paired-bootstrap confidence intervals.
5. Robust gates use one deterministic shuffled opening order with disjoint offsets across shards.
6. Lower static loss, lower teacher MAE/RMSE, larger networks and stronger teachers do **not** imply higher Elo.
7. Runtime-exact QAT or direct discrete optimization is required for new NNU4/NNU5 training work.
8. Avoid adding runtime architecture cost unless a non-zero learned signal is demonstrated first.
9. Do not mix architecture, teacher, search and objective changes in one causal experiment.
10. No v5 candidate is promoted without a direct large gate against v5.06.

## Current direction

Stop local NNUE micro-surgery for this root-probe signal. The next experiments should change the mechanism rather than increase the local perturbation budget: prioritize **search-side correction/history or pruning-aware residuals**, or a globally trained representation/objective that can reorganize L2 jointly. Keep v5.06 as the direct v5 baseline and retain runtime-exact QAT as required infrastructure.
